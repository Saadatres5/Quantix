# QuantixTools — Free Islamic Tools

QuantixTools offers a free Zakat calculator and a Halal food E-number checker. Calculations run in your browser. The information is for education and reference, not a fatwa; consult a qualified scholar for personal rulings.

- [Zakat Calculator](https://quantixtools.com/zakat-calculator/) — Estimate Zakat on cash, gold, silver, crypto, stocks, and business assets.
- [Halal E-Number Checker](https://quantixtools.com/halal-food-checker/) — Search more than 100 food additives and review their listed status and source notes.
- [Barcode Scanner](https://quantixtools.com/halal-food-checker/scanner/)
