About QuantixTools

Free Islamic tools for Muslims worldwide — built with accuracy, transparency, and care.

Our Mission

QuantixTools was created with a simple mission: to provide free, accurate, and easy-to-use Islamic tools that help Muslims in their daily lives. We focus on two areas where good online tools are genuinely rare — Zakat calculation and Halal food additive information.

We are based in the UAE and deeply understand the needs of Muslim communities both in the Gulf region and worldwide. Our tools are available in English and Arabic to serve the widest possible audience.

Our Methodology

Zakat Calculations

Our Zakat calculator follows the methodology of the majority of contemporary Islamic scholars, including those at the Islamic Fiqh Academy, AAOIFI (Accounting and Auditing Organization for Islamic Financial Institutions), and the National Zakat Foundation (UK). We use the silver Nisab by default as recommended by Sheikh Yusuf al-Qaradawi and the European Council for Fatwa and Research.

Halal Food Information

Our E-number database is compiled from EU food additive regulations cross-referenced with published halal guidance from JAKIM (Malaysia), IFANCA (USA), Halal Food Authority (UK), and ESMA (UAE). Where scholars differ, we present the most cautious position and clearly explain the disagreement.

Our Principles

  • Free forever: All tools on QuantixTools are and will remain free
  • Privacy first: We never store your financial data. Calculations happen in your browser
  • Transparent sources: We cite our sources so you can verify our information
  • Honest limitations: We are clear about what we know and when to consult a scholar
  • Multilingual: Arabic and English to serve the global Muslim community
