Is E422 (Glycerin) Halal?

◆ Mashbooh (Verify Source)

What is E422?

E422, commonly known as glycerin or glycerol, is a sweet-tasting compound used as a humectant (moisture retainer), sweetener, and preservative in food and pharmaceuticals.

Source & Production

Glycerin can be produced from either plant sources (palm oil, coconut oil, soybean oil, vegetable oils) or animal sources (beef or pork fat). Modern production increasingly uses plant sources, but manufacturer practices vary.

Halal Status

Status: Mashbooh (Doubtful)

E422 halal status depends entirely on its source. Plant-derived glycerin is halal, but animal-derived glycerin (especially from pork) is haram. Most reputable manufacturers now use plant sources, but verification is essential.

How to Verify E422 is Halal

  • Look for halal certification marks (JAKIM, ESMA, HFA, IFANCA)
  • Check product labels for "vegetable glycerin" specification
  • Contact the manufacturer to confirm source
  • Buy from halal-certified brands

Products Containing E422

  • Sugar-free confectionery
  • Ice cream
  • Cough syrups
  • Toothpaste and mouthwash
  • Cosmetics
  • Processed meats
  • Baked goods
