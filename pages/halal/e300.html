Is E300 (Ascorbic Acid / Vitamin C) Halal?

✓ Halal

What is E300?

E300 is ascorbic acid, chemically identical to Vitamin C. It is used as a preservative and antioxidant to maintain freshness and nutritional quality in food and beverages.

Source & Production

E300 is typically derived from plant sources, most commonly corn glucose or through bacterial fermentation. Modern production methods yield a pure form of ascorbic acid suitable for food fortification.

Halal Status

Status: Halal

E300 is completely halal because it is plant-derived and contains no animal products.

Common Uses

  • Preserved meats (as antioxidant)
  • Soft drinks and juices
  • Bread and baked goods
  • Canned vegetables
  • Oils and fats
  • Beer and wine production
  • Fortified breakfast cereals

Health Benefits

E300 adds nutritional value by providing Vitamin C, which:

  • Supports immune function
  • Acts as an antioxidant
  • Helps collagen formation
  • Improves iron absorption
