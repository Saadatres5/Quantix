Is E150 (Caramel) Halal or Haram?

✓ Usually Halal (Verify)

What is E150?

E150 (Caramel color) is a dark brown color produced by heating sugars until they caramelize. It is one of the oldest food colorings and is widely used internationally.

Source & Production

E150 is typically produced from plant-derived sugars (cane sugar, beet sugar, or high fructose corn syrup). The sugar is heated, sometimes with ammonia or sulfites, to create the brown color.

Halal Status

Status: Usually Halal

Since E150 is typically plant-derived, most Islamic scholars consider it halal. However, the exact production process can vary by manufacturer.

Products Containing E150

  • Cola and dark soft drinks
  • Dark beers
  • Sauces and condiments
  • Vinegars
  • Baked goods
  • Chocolate products
