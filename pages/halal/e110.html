Is E110 (Sunset Yellow) Halal or Haram?

✓ Halal

What is E110?

E110, also known as Sunset Yellow or Yellow Orange S, is a synthetic yellow-orange azo dye widely used in food and beverage production throughout Europe and internationally.

Halal Status

Status: Halal

E110 is considered halal because it is entirely synthetic with no animal-derived ingredients. It is approved by major halal certification bodies.

Common Uses

  • Soft drinks and beverages
  • Confectionery and sweets
  • Baked goods
  • Sauces and condiments
  • Dairy products
