Is E621 (MSG / Monosodium Glutamate) Halal?

◆ Mashbooh (Verify Source)

What is E621?

E621, commonly known as MSG or monosodium glutamate, is a flavor enhancer that amplifies savory tastes. It is the sodium salt of glutamic acid.

Source & Production

E621 is typically produced through fermentation of plant-based materials such as tapioca, sugarcane molasses, or corn. However, the exact production method and source varies by manufacturer.

Halal Status

Status: Mashbooh (Doubtful)

While E621 itself is not animal-derived, the source and production method vary. Most modern MSG is plant-derived and halal, but you should verify through halal certification on the packaging.

How to Verify E621 is Halal

  • Look for halal certification logos (JAKIM, ESMA, HFA, IFANCA)
  • Check the manufacturer's website for halal certification
  • Contact the manufacturer directly to confirm source
  • Look for labels stating "plant-based" or "fermented from tapioca"

Products Containing E621

  • Instant noodles and ramen
  • Soup cubes and instant soup
  • Soy sauce and other sauces
  • Snack foods and crisps
  • Processed meats
  • Asian condiments
  • Seasoning powders
