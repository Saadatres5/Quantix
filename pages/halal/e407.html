Is E407 (Carrageenan) Halal?

✓ Halal

What is E407?

E407, commonly known as carrageenan, is a natural thickener extracted from red seaweed species such as Irish moss (Chondrus crispus). It forms a gel when added to food.

Source & Production

Carrageenan is harvested from seaweed, dried, and then extracted through alkaline processing. The result is a plant-based gelling agent containing no animal products.

Halal Status

Status: Halal

E407 is completely halal because it is derived entirely from seaweed (a plant source) with no animal-derived ingredients.

Common Uses

  • Ice cream
  • Puddings and desserts
  • Yogurt and dairy products
  • Salad dressings
  • Processed meats
  • Beverages
  • Pet food
  • Toothpaste
