Is E102 (Tartrazine) Halal or Haram?

✓ Halal (Generally)

What is E102 (Tartrazine)?

E102, commonly known as Tartrazine or Yellow No. 5, is a synthetic yellow azo dye used extensively in food and beverage production. It is one of the most widely used artificial food colorings in Europe and internationally.

Source & Production

E102 is a synthetic colourant. A synthetic source alone does not establish the status of every finished product; check product-specific ingredients and follow guidance from a qualified scholar or trusted halal-certification body.

Halal Status of E102

QuantixTools classification: Halal

This classification reflects the additive's synthetic source. Product formulations and certification standards may differ, so check the finished product and follow the guidance you trust.

  • Review product-specific ingredient and certification information
  • Ask the manufacturer if the source or processing is unclear
  • Follow guidance from a qualified scholar or trusted halal-certification body

Products Containing E102

E102 is commonly found in:

  • Soft drinks and energy drinks (especially lemon and orange flavored beverages)
  • Confectionery and sweets
  • Ice cream and frozen desserts
  • Yogurt and dairy products
  • Jams and preserves
  • Soups and sauces
  • Baked goods and snacks
  • Medicinal syrups

Health Considerations

Some individuals may be sensitive to tartrazine. Check product labels and consult a healthcare professional if you have concerns or experience a reaction:

  • Check product labels for tartrazine or E102
  • Follow any warnings relevant to your known sensitivities

If you have known sensitivities to tartrazine, look for products explicitly labeled as tartrazine-free.
