Is E102 (Tartrazine) Halal or Haram?

✓ Halal (Generally)

What is E102 (Tartrazine)?

E102, commonly known as Tartrazine or Yellow No. 5, is a synthetic yellow azo dye used extensively in food and beverage production. It is one of the most widely used artificial food colorings in Europe and internationally.

Source & Production

E102 is entirely synthetic and does not contain any animal-derived ingredients. It is produced through chemical synthesis from coal tar derivatives or from modern petroleum-based chemical processes. Because of its purely synthetic nature, there are no halal concerns regarding its production process or ingredients.

Halal Status of E102

Ruling: Generally Halal

The vast majority of Islamic scholars consider E102 (Tartrazine) to be halal because:

  • It is completely synthetic and not derived from any haram (forbidden) sources
  • No animal products are used in its manufacture
  • It does not contain any alcohol or prohibited substances
  • It is approved by major halal certification bodies including ESMA, JAKIM, and HFA

Products Containing E102

E102 is commonly found in:

  • Soft drinks and energy drinks (especially lemon and orange flavored beverages)
  • Confectionery and sweets
  • Ice cream and frozen desserts
  • Yogurt and dairy products
  • Jams and preserves
  • Soups and sauces
  • Baked goods and snacks
  • Medicinal syrups

Health Considerations

While E102 is halal, it's worth noting that some individuals may have sensitivities to tartrazine:

  • Allergic reactions: Some people experience allergic symptoms including urticaria and angioedema
  • Hyperactivity: In sensitive children, tartrazine may trigger behavioral issues
  • Asthma: May exacerbate asthma symptoms in susceptible individuals

If you have known sensitivities to tartrazine, look for products explicitly labeled as tartrazine-free.
