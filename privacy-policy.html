Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. Introduction

QuantixTools ("we", "our", "us") operates quantixtools.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website.

2. Information We Collect

Information You Do NOT Provide

Our Zakat calculator processes all calculations entirely within your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit any financial information you enter into our calculators. Your Zakat data never leaves your device.

Automatically Collected Information

Our hosting provider may process standard server-log data, such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, and request time, to deliver and secure the Site. We do not currently use Google Analytics or another website analytics service.

3. Cookies and Browser Storage

Google AdSense and its enabled advertising partners may use cookies for ad delivery and measurement. Our language selector stores your preference in your browser's local storage, not in a cookie.

You can manage or clear cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Some site features may not function correctly if browser storage is disabled.

Our website contains links to external sites (Islamic charities, certification bodies, government pages). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these sites. Please review their privacy policies independently.

6. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete any personal data we hold about you. Contact us at hello@quantixtools.com to exercise these rights.
